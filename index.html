These tools lie fluently.

The failure that costs a design team is the confident wrong answer: plausible, free of warnings and ready to ship. A wrong number arrives looking exactly like a right one, and review practice built for human mistakes does not flag it.

A deterministic verification layer catches it, where a smarter model would not. The model proposes, the engine decides, and nothing ships on a model's say-so.

AI does not replace the design engineer.

It changes how much one engineer can run and check: sweep and statistical campaigns at cube scale, requirements work in bulk, decks and documentation rendered and checked.
